01

The mark

A rune is a mark carved to name a thing. Ours is cut the way a stone carver cuts a letter: each wall of the groove slopes down to the centerline, and the light picks out the walls.

One angle
Every branch runs at 30 degrees. That makes each half of the rune an equilateral triangle, and the same line cuts the top and bottom of the stave.
Eleven facets
The outline slopes to its centerline like a V-cut groove. Light comes from the upper left, so walls facing it are lit and walls facing away are deep.
Flat when small
Below 32 pixels the facets go and the silhouette stays. The favicon is always flat.
Ochre or Ink
The rune is Ochre, or Ink in one-color work, or Paper on an Ochre field. Nothing else.

02

Lockups

Four arrangements, each in color, one-color Ink and reversed. The horizontal lockup is the default. Use the others when the space asks for them.

03

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space is x on every side, where x is a quarter of the mark's height: the depth of one branch. Nothing else goes inside it, including the edge of the page.

05

Color

Light only. Every color is set explicitly and there is no dark theme. Ochre is the red-brown rubbed into carved runestones; use it for the mark, links and one button, and let Paper do the rest. Contrast is the WCAG 2 ratio, measured.

Facet tones, the mark only. Lit #c25a33, light #ad4925, Ochre #9c3d1c, deep #7a2d14. Carved in light on Ochre: #ffffff, #fbf4ef, #efd9cc, #d9b19c. They never carry text and never appear outside the rune.

07

Status words

The product's vocabulary. They appear exactly as written, lowercase, always in a chip. Green is only for open, so a reader learns that green means the registry said no record.

open
The registry has no record, and a registrar confirmed the standard price.
open*
The registry has no record. Price not verified.
premium
No record, but priced well above standard.
reserved
No record, but no registrar will sell it.
parked
Registered and parked at a domain-sale service.
expiring
Registered, in redemption or pending delete.
taken
Registered.
unknown
No usable registry answer. Never counted as open.

08

Voice

The Norse register stays in the name and the mark. Everywhere else the product speaks plainly, because plain is the whole point.

  • Plain. Short sentences. The words a builder would use to a friend. If a sentence needs a second read, rewrite it.
  • Says what it checked. Every claim names its source. Never round a guess up to a fact.
  • Dry, not cute. One light line is fine. Puns on "rune" stay out of product copy.
  • No hype words. Not revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, seamless or effortless.
  • No em-dashes. Commas, colons and periods.
  • Names are invented whole for each surface, and checked before they appear with a status.
